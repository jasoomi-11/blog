🏰 TH8 to TH10 – Mid-Level Madness Begins!

Welcome to the **TH8–TH10 zone** — where things get serious, but also a little chaotic 😏. If you thought TH5–7 was fun, buckle up! Here, you’ll unlock new troops, advanced spells, upgraded heroes, and base layouts that can make or break your attacks.

In this guide, we’re taking you through **everything from TH8 to TH10**: upgrade priorities, lab roadmaps, attack combos, farming tricks, war strategies, and base design tips. Basically, we’ve got your back whether you’re a trophy hunter, a farming fanatic, or just someone who loves smashing villages 😎🛡️.

So grab your **Barbarians, Wizards, and Lightning spells**, because we’re about to dive deep into mid-level Clash of Clans madness! 🚀⚔️

🚀 TH8 Progression Roadmap

Welcome to TH8 — the level where your village stops being “cute” and starts being “dangerous” 😎. Proper upgrade order is key, otherwise your troops will cry while your defenses get demolished! TH8 is where mid-game strategies start to shine: mix farming efficiency with war readiness to dominate both loot and trophies.

🏗️ Upgrade Priorities

Mid-level Clash is all about smart planning. Focus first on upgrades that give the **most immediate impact**:

  • Lab Upgrades: Archers, Barbarians, and Giants first — faster attacks and stronger defenses make farming and wars easier.
  • Defenses: Cannons, Archer Towers, and Wizard Towers. Mix splash and single-target defenses to survive clan attacks.
  • Heroes: Barbarian King. Upgrade gradually, but make sure he’s never idle — his abilities save more loot than walls sometimes 😏.
  • Clan Castle: Upgrade it early to get bigger donation troops — your war attacks will thank you!

🧱 Wall Priorities

Walls may seem boring, but they’re your “stop-n-my-village” first line of defense. Upgrade in chunks of 5–10 to see noticeable improvement. Don’t waste resources on scattered single walls — concentrated upgrades actually delay attackers more effectively.

⚔️ Farming vs War Upgrade Path

Focus Upgrade Tips Why It Matters
Farming Collectors & Storage → Troops → Walls → Heroes Maximize loot while keeping attacks fast, cheap, and fun 💰
War Defenses → Lab → Clan Castle → Heroes → Walls Protect trophies, dominate clan points, and stop enemy raids 🛡️

Tip: Balance is key! Don’t rush defenses at the expense of your troops or lab upgrades. Overleveled defenses with weak attacks make you a sitting duck in both farming and wars 😅. A well-planned TH8 can handle mid-level wars and loot collectors like a pro.

Extra tip: Always plan your upgrades around your playtime. Upgrade heroes or defenses while you’re offline to reduce wasted resources. And remember — walls may be slow, but upgraded walls frustrate enemies more than anything else.

🌾 TH8 Farming Army Guide

Farming at TH8 is all about **maximizing loot without draining your pockets** 😏💰. Speed, efficiency, and smart targeting are your best friends. Forget style points — the goal is **stuffed storages** and a smiling clan chest.

💸 Low-Cost Elixir Armies

  • Giants + Archers: Reliable, cheap, perfect for raiding collectors and storage buildings.
  • Barbarians + Archers (BARCH): Fast, disposable army — ignore defenses, grab loot, dash 🏃‍♂️💨.
  • Giants + Wizards: Slightly pricier, but excellent for mid-level bases with exposed storages.
  • Giants + Goblins: Fun mix — tank the defenses, sneak in for loot. Goblins are basically “thieves in armor.”

🖤 Dark Elixir Farming Tips

  • Focus on **exposed drills** — don’t waste troops on hidden treasures or full bases unless it’s worth it.
  • Use **Barbarians, Archers, or Minions** to grab DE quickly before defenses react.
  • Lightning Spells for **DE drills only** — walls aren’t worth burning your spell stash 😅.
  • Scout bases carefully. A high DE storage hidden under walls is rarely worth a risky raid.

💰 Loot Maximizing Strategies

Strategy Army Composition Tips
Fast Collector Raids Barbarians + Archers Ignore defenses, hit as many collectors as possible 🚀
Storage Targeting Giants + Wizards + Archers Clear outer buildings first, then focus on storage 🏦
DE Drills Grab Minions + Lightning Spells Hit exposed drills quickly before enemy troops respond ⚡
Hybrid Loot Push Giants + BARCH + Heal Spells Good balance of speed, safety, and loot efficiency 🛡️

Pro Tip: Don’t overthink it! Fast raids and simple armies often outperform expensive compositions. Keep your heroes busy, monitor loot availability, and always scout before committing — that’s how TH8 farmers become legends 😎💎.

Extra tip: Use **seasonal events** to double resources whenever possible. Even a few quick BARCH raids can fill storages faster than weeks of slow farming. Remember: smart farming = happy troops, full storages, and a stress-free TH8 life!

⚔️ TH8 War Attack Strategies

Welcome to **war mode**! 🏆 At TH8, your attacks can make or break your clan’s victory. The trick? Smart troop combos, spell timing, and knowing when to **send heroes on a rampage** 😏. Always plan your attack based on enemy base layout, not just your favorite army.

2-Star Safe Attacks

  • Giants + Wizards: Tank with Giants, splash with Wizards. Reliable, low-risk for consistent 2 stars.
  • Barch for Corner Buildings: Barbarians + Archers clean outside structures while main push handles the core. Great for keeping war stars safe.
  • Giants + Wall Breakers + Archers: Slowly funnel troops in for corner destruction — safe and efficient.
  • Remember: Sometimes **2 stars is better than a failed 3-star attempt**! Don’t be greedy 💀.

3-Star Attack Combos

  • GoWiPe (Golems + Wizards + P.E.K.K.A): Classic TH8 3-star combo. Golems soak damage, Wizards melt defenses, P.E.K.K.A crushes the core 💥.
  • Hog Riders + Heal Spells: Stealthy and deadly. Always target splash defenses first to avoid nasty traps 🐖⚡.
  • Balloon + Lightning Combo: Take out Wizard Towers and Air Defenses before your main push. Air supremacy ftw! ☁️🔥
  • GiBarch + Heroes: Use Giants to tank, Barch for cleanup, and heroes to secure the core. Good for mixed 2–3 star attacks.

Clan War Attack Tips

Tip Why It Works
Scout the Base First Trap locations, defensive traps, and weak spots are revealed 👀
Use Heroes Wisely Barbarian King and Archer Queen can secure extra stars if timed right 🦸
Spell Timing is Everything Heal, Rage, and Freeze can turn a 2-star into a 3-star if used correctly ⚡
Communicate with Clanmates Coordinated attacks often outperform solo hero charges 🙌
Funnel Your Troops Proper funneling ensures troops don’t wander and attack the wrong section of the base 🌀

Pro Tip: Practice makes perfect! War attacks at TH8 are fun, but mistakes are costly. Plan, scout, and laugh at your failed attacks 😅. Bonus tip: Watch replays of top TH8 war attacks to see how pros handle tricky layouts — learning from mistakes costs nothing!

🛡️ TH8 Base Design Guide

Welcome to the world of **walls, traps, and defense madness**! 🏰 A strong base can make enemies rage-quit, while a weak one… well, your loot disappears faster than cookies at a sleepover 😅. At TH8, layout creativity starts to matter — small tweaks can drastically reduce enemy success.

🏹 Anti-3 Star War Base Layouts

  • Keep your Clan Castle centrally located — ensures enemy troops trigger defensive troops first.
  • Air Defenses should be near core, not corners, to stop sneak Dragon or Balloon attacks ☁️🔥.
  • Spread splash defenses like Wizard Towers and Mortars to prevent mass troop wipes.
  • Use double-layered walls around key defenses — slows down P.E.K.K.A or Giant pushes.

🌾 Farming Base vs War Base Differences

Base Type Layout Focus Tip
Farming Base Protect storages Walls around Gold/Elixir, trophies not important 🏦. Place collectors outside to bait attackers.
War Base Protect Town Hall & defenses Spread out defenses, make funneling hard. Test with friendly challenges ⚔️

💣 Trap Placement Tips

  • Place **Giant Bombs** between splash defenses to surprise Hog Riders 🐖💥.
  • **Spring Traps** on likely funneling paths — predict troop movement 😏.
  • **Air Traps** near Air Defenses to counter Balloon/Dragon attacks ☁️⚡.
  • Use **Hidden Teslas** to lure enemy troops into overkill spots for maximum chaos ⚡🔋.

Pro Tip: Don’t copy layouts blindly! Test your base with friendly challenges first. A single well-placed wall or trap can turn a potential 3-star into a sad 1-star 😎. Extra tip: Rotate your layout every few weeks to confuse enemy scouting and reduce war star losses.

Quick Hack: Combine farming and war base principles — protect core resources while funneling attackers away from your defenses. Smart placement + strategic traps = a TH8 base that’s both loot-friendly and war-ready 🏰💪.

👑 TH8 Hero Usage & Upgrade

At TH8, your hero — the mighty Barbarian King — becomes a game-changer ⚔️. Used wisely, he can secure extra stars in war, protect loot in farming, or just look intimidating while wrecking villages 😎.

🦸 Barbarian King Priority

  • First Upgrade: Level him gradually to unlock stronger abilities.
  • Farming Use: Deploy him to tank defenses while your Barch army sweeps collectors 🌾.
  • War Use: Send him with core-breaking troops to secure those crucial 2-3 stars 🏆.

⏰ Upgrade Timing for War vs Farming

Focus When to Upgrade Why
Farming Upgrade between attacks Max loot gain while hero is unavailable temporarily 💰
War Upgrade only outside Clan War days Never leave your King inactive during wars ⚔️

⚔️ Hero Attack Combos

  • King + Giants: Tank through outer defenses while Giants soak hits.
  • King + Wizards: Use King to distract defenses, Wizards to melt them fast 🔥.
  • Tip: Don’t send the King alone unless you enjoy watching him get roasted 😅.

Pro Tip: Upgrade wisely, deploy smartly, and your Barbarian King will make TH8 attacks **fun and effective**. Remember: heroes aren’t just for show — they are your **secret weapon of chaos** 😏👑.

✨ TH8 Spell Guide & Timing

Spells at TH8 are your secret sauce 🍯 for winning attacks. Use them wisely and you’ll turn a risky raid into a loot-filled victory, misuse them and… well, your troops might just look cute while dying 😅.

💥 Rage, Heal, Lightning Usage

  • Rage Spell: Boosts troop damage and speed. Perfect when your army hits the core 💪⚡.
  • Heal Spell: Keep your Giants, Barbarians, or Hogs alive in heavy defense zones ❤️.
  • Lightning Spell: Take out enemy Air Defenses or Wizard Towers from a distance ⚡🏰.

📍 Best Spell Placement for Attacks

  • Rage spells: Place right before the core of defenses for max destruction.
  • Heal spells: Drop where splash damage is high — Wizard Towers, Mortars.
  • Lightning: Always aim for clustered defenses or high-value buildings for maximum efficiency.

⚠️ Avoid Common Spell Mistakes

Mistake Why It Fails Better Strategy
Dropping Rage too early Troops may go off path and die outside the core 😅 Wait until main defenses are in range
Heal on wrong units Barbs/Wizards die too fast, spell wasted Focus on tank units like Giants or Hogs 🛡️
Lightning on empty or scattered buildings Minimal damage, wasted spell 💀 Target high-value or clustered defenses

Pro Tip: Spells are your secret weapon. Timing beats quantity. Master the spell game and your TH8 attacks will feel like magic ✨⚔️.

❌ TH8 Common Mistakes & Tips

At TH8, it’s easy to make mistakes that cost loot, trophies, or even your sanity 😅. Don’t worry — we’ve compiled the most common pitfalls and how to avoid them. Consider this your “don’t be a noob” checklist ✅.

🧱 Wall Over-Upgrading

  • Many players spend too much Elixir on walls early — they look pretty, but your troops and defenses suffer 😭.
  • Tip: Upgrade walls gradually, focus on **core walls first**, then outer layers.

⚔️ Wrong Lab Upgrades

  • Some rush expensive troops first, leaving main attack combos weak.
  • Tip: Prioritize troops used in your main farming & war strategies, like Barbarians, Archers, Giants, and Wizards.

🛡️ Poor Attack Strategy

  • Sending all troops blindly or misusing spells = disaster 😭.
  • Tip: Scout bases first, deploy in waves, and use spells at the **right moment**.

💡 Additional Tips

Mistake How to Fix
Neglecting Clan Castle troops Request reinforcements and use them strategically in attacks 🦸
Ignoring Builder Management Keep at least one builder free for important upgrades
Skipping Hero Upgrades Upgrade Barbarian King gradually to maintain attack strength 👑

Pro Tip: Learn from your fails! Every mistake teaches you how to farm smarter, attack better, and design stronger bases. TH8 is all about balance — **loot, defense, and fun** 😎💥.

🚀 TH9 Progression Roadmap

Welcome to **TH9**, the land of chaos, X-Bows, and powerful heroes 🏰💥. At this stage, you’ve got more troops, bigger defenses, and a bigger headache if you don’t plan your upgrades wisely 😅.

🔧 Upgrade Priority: X-Bows, Heroes, Lab

  • X-Bows: Place them early — they are your long-range death machines 🏹.
  • Heroes: Archer Queen & Barbarian King are crucial for war attacks and farming.
  • Lab: Upgrade your main attack troops first — GoWiPe, Dragons, and Hogs 🐉🐖.

💰 Dark Elixir Saving Strategies

  • Don’t blow all your DE on troops — prioritize hero upgrades.
  • Farm DE with smart combos like Giants + Minions or Barch to slowly stack your reserves 🌾.
  • Check your Clanmates for donated troops to save DE for attacks 🦸‍♂️.

⚔️ Farming vs War Path

Focus Strategy Tip
Farming Upgrade resource buildings, main troops Keep heroes for attacks, loot efficiently 💰
War Upgrade defenses, heroes, lab troops Delay farming upgrades temporarily to strengthen war attacks ⚔️

Pro Tip: TH9 is a balancing act — don’t rush walls, save DE, and plan each upgrade. Follow the roadmap and you’ll be the **queen/king of TH9 attacks** in no time 😎👑.

🌾 TH9 Farming Army Strategies

At TH9, farming isn’t just about collecting loot — it’s an art 🎨. Deploy the right army, scout your target, and don’t let DE or Elixir slip through your fingers 😏.

🛡️ GoWiPe & Mass Dragons

  • GoWiPe (Golems + Wizards + P.E.K.K.A): Perfect for high-value bases. Golems tank, Wizards destroy, P.E.K.K.A breaks the core 💥.
  • Mass Dragons: Easy to farm high loot bases. Use Rage/Heal wisely, target Air Defenses first ☁️🔥.

💰 BARCH Tips (Barbs + Archers)

  • Cheap and fast, ideal for collector farming.
  • Spread troops to avoid splash defenses and maximize loot 🌾.
  • Use heroes to tank for Barbs and Archers when farming DE 😎.

🔍 Base Scouting for Loot

  • Look for bases with full collectors and weak defenses.
  • Check for traps and Clan Castle troops — avoid surprise losses ⚠️.
  • Prioritize DE storages for hero upgrades 💎.

Pro Tip: Rotate armies depending on target — sometimes BARCH is faster, sometimes GoWiPe or Dragons maximize loot. Smart farming = faster progress 😏🚀.

⚔️ TH9 Clan War Attack Strategies

TH9 war attacks are **all about precision, heroes, and combos** 🏆. Miss a step and your 3-star plan becomes a 1-star disaster 😅.

👑 Queen Walk Attacks

  • Use Archer Queen with healers to take out key defenses safely.
  • Start on one side to create a funnel, then deploy main army ⚔️.
  • Tip: Keep healers alive until core is engaged for max impact 🌟.

☁️ LavaLoon vs GoWiPe Strategy

  • LavaLoon: Lava Hounds tank while Balloons destroy defenses.
  • GoWiPe: Golems soak damage, Wizards and P.E.K.K.A break through core.
  • Choose based on base layout and enemy Clan Castle troops 🏰.

🏆 3-Star War Plan

  • Scout first, identify traps and key defenses.
  • Use heroes at critical moments to secure the last stars 👑⚡.
  • Deploy spells smartly — a misplaced Rage or Freeze can ruin the attack!

Pro Tip: Practice attacks in friendly challenges — **a perfect attack needs timing, planning, and a little luck 😏**.

🛡️ TH9 Base Building Tips

Base design at TH9 can make attackers cry 😢 or rage-quit 😎. From Anti-3 star layouts to smart Inferno/X-Bow placement, your base is your silent warrior.

🏹 Anti-3 Star War Base

  • Centralize Town Hall and key defenses.
  • Spread traps strategically to surprise Hog Riders and Giants 🐖💥.
  • Use double-layer walls around storages for extra protection 🧱.

☁️ Air Defense Positioning

  • Place Air Defenses away from edges to prevent easy Loon/Dragon attacks.
  • Cover Wizard Towers to counter mass troops 🌟.

💣 Inferno/X-Bow Layout Guide

  • Inferno Towers: Protect core or high-value targets — single-target for heroes, multi-target for mass troops 🔥.
  • X-Bows: Choose ground or ground+air mode based on attack trends.
  • Tip: Mixing layout types confuses attackers and maximizes survival ⚔️.

Pro Tip: Test your layout with friendly challenges before war — a well-placed defense is worth a thousand troops 😏🛡️.

👑 TH9 Hero Upgrade & Usage

TH9 heroes are **game-changers**. The Archer Queen and Barbarian King can turn a lost battle into a glorious 3-star attack 😎.

🦸 Queen + King Upgrade Priorities

  • Upgrade Archer Queen first if focusing on war attacks — her ability can solo parts of bases 🏹.
  • Barbarian King is great for farming and tanking — upgrade gradually.
  • Timing is crucial: never leave both heroes down during Clan War 🏆.

⚡ Hero Ability Timing

  • Use Queen’s ability to clear core defenses or high-value storages.
  • King’s ability is best for tanking and pushing through the enemy funnel.
  • Tip: Wait for heavy splash damage areas to activate abilities for maximum efficiency 💥.

🤝 Hero Combos for War Attacks

  • Queen + Giants: Queen clears outer defenses while Giants tank inside ⚔️.
  • King + Wizards: King distracts, Wizards melt defenses quickly 🔥.
  • Both heroes together in core: Perfect for high-value 3-star attacks 👑⚡.

Pro Tip: Upgrade heroes strategically and deploy smartly — they are your **secret TH9 superpower** 😏.

✨ TH9 Spell & Siege Machine Guide

Spells and siege machines are the ultimate helpers in TH9 ⚡. Used correctly, they can make armies unstoppable; used poorly, they just look cool while dying 😅.

💥 Jump, Rage, Freeze Timing

  • Jump Spell: Allows troops to bypass walls — perfect for core penetration 🧱.
  • Rage Spell: Boosts troop damage and speed inside critical zones ⚡.
  • Freeze Spell: Stops infernos, X-Bows, or high-damage defenses temporarily ❄️.

🏰 Siege Machine Attack Tips

  • Wall Wrecker: Target core, paired with heroes for max damage 🚀.
  • Battle Blimp: Drop directly on the Town Hall for quick 2-3 stars 🏹.
  • Stone Slammer: Air-based attacks need smart deployment to take down defenses ☁️🔥.

📍 Spell Placement for Funneling

  • Use Rage and Heal in clusters where troops are about to face splash damage 💪.
  • Freeze high-value defenses right before troops enter for max effect 🛡️.
  • Jump spells for a clear path to core — avoid wasted troop wandering 😏.

Pro Tip: **Spells and siege machines can win battles alone** if timed right. TH9 is all about coordination — use brains + troops + magic ⚡🔥.

Even at TH9, players make silly mistakes. Avoid these and you’ll save loot, trophies, and sanity 😅.

  • Tip: Upgrade walls gradually after main defenses and troops are upgraded.

🚀 TH10 Progression Roadmap

Welcome to **TH10**, the level where chaos meets strategy 😎. Inferno Towers, X-Bows, and a huge Lab are your new toys — and your new headache 😅.

🔧 Upgrade Priority: Inferno Towers, X-Bow, Lab

  • X-Bows: Place them in strategic spots; choose ground or ground+air depending on your attack trends 🏹.
  • Lab Upgrades: Focus on main attack troops like GoWiWi, Hogs, and Bowlers before fancy stuff 🐖⚡.

💰 Dark Elixir & Resource Management

  • Save DE for heroes and key troops — don’t spend on silly low-level upgrades 😏.
  • Upgrade collectors and storages gradually to keep your loot flowing 🌾.
  • Tip: Check Clan Castle donations to save DE for attacks 🦸‍♂️.

⚔️ War vs Farming Priorities

Focus Priority Tip
Farming Resource buildings, troops, heroes Efficiently gather loot, save DE 💎
