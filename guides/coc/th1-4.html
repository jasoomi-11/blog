🏰 Town Hall 1 – Your Tiny Kingdom Begins!

Welcome to Town Hall 1 — the adorable baby stage of Clash of Clans 😄. At this point, your village looks like a “starter pack” version of a real base, and that’s okay! Everyone starts somewhere… even the pros once had this cute-looking TH1 village.

✨ What You Unlock at TH1

  • 🏡 Town Hall – The tiny wooden box that controls everything.
  • 👷‍♂️ 1 Builder – Yes, just one guy… he works hard, be nice to him.
  • 💰 Gold Storage + Gold Mine – Your money-making machines.
  • 🧪 Elixir Storage + Elixir Collector – The purple juice factory.
  • 🏹 1 Cannon – Your only defense… please don’t expect miracles.
  • 💂 Barracks – The place that gives you your first troop.

⚔️ Troops Available at TH1

You get only one troop at this stage:

  • 👨‍🌾 Barbarian – The yellow-haired angry boy. Cheap, loyal, and always screaming.

📚 Beginner Tip

Don’t stress! TH1 lasts only a few minutes — upgrade everything, enjoy the cuteness, and move to TH2 quickly 🚀. The real game starts later… this is just the tutorial playground 🎮.

🏠 Town Hall 1 – Ultimate Beginner Start

Welcome to Town Hall 1 – the stage where your entire Clash journey begins. You're basically the “level 1 hero” in every RPG… cute, weak, and extremely excited. 😄 TH1 is simple, tiny, and honestly feels like Supercell is just saying: “Here, take these buildings and don't mess anything up… yet.”

⏳ Best First 24 Hours Strategy

  • 🔧 Upgrade your Gold Storage + Elixir Storage first. Because trust me… you'll fill them faster than you expect.
  • ⚔️ Build and upgrade your Barracks immediately. You need troops fast — nobody wants to sit at TH1 with 10 Barbarians crying for war.
  • 🏡 Place all buildings the moment they unlock. Even if they look useless (like Builder's Hut that just stands there judging you).
  • 💰 Attack instantly. Your army trains so fast at TH1 that you’ll feel like a machine gun of Barbarians.

Basically, your first day is simple: spam upgrades, spam attacks, spam happiness. You can literally max TH1 in a few hours if you stay active.

🚀 Why Rushing TH1–2 is Actually Good

Beginners always ask: “Should I rush from TH1 to TH2 fast?” And the answer is: YES, 100%! 💨

TH1 has:

  • ❌ No Heroes
  • ❌ No Spells
  • ❌ No Walls worth crying about
  • ❌ Only 1 real army option

So staying at TH1 for long is like being stuck in the game tutorial forever. Supercell expects you to zoom to TH2 because the real game starts there.

Rushing TH1 → TH2 is not bad… it’s smart gameplay. 😎

💎 Early Game Loot Management Tips

  • 🟣 Don’t let your storages stay full. Full storage = wasted loot. And wasted loot = instant heartbreak.
  • 🔨 Always keep at least one builder working. Idle builder = emotional damage.
  • ⚔️ Attack low-level abandoned bases. These bases are loot mines — and they never fight back. Perfect for beginners. 😁
  • 💡 Upgrade cheap buildings early. Low cost + short time = fast progression.
  • 📈 Save Elixir for troop upgrades. Your Barbarians are your only friends right now… keep them strong.

TH1 is basically the warm-up stage. Enjoy it, upgrade everything quickly, grab loot like a greedy Goblin 🥷💰 and jump to **TH2** as soon as you're done.

🏰 Town Hall 2 – Your Village Learns to Walk!

Welcome to Town Hall 2 — the stage where your tiny village finally stops looking like a lost tutorial island. Things are still simple, cute, and a little slow… kind of like a baby taking its first steps. But don’t worry — this is where your village starts feeling “real.”

✨ What You Unlock at TH2

  • 🏹 Archer Tower – Your first defender with actual accuracy (unlike the Cannon’s “spray and pray”).
  • 🧱 More Walls – Finally, your base doesn’t look “half open.”
  • 💥 Bomb – A tiny surprise gift for attackers. Boom = happiness.
  • ⛏️ More Collectors – More gold, more elixir, more progress!
  • 👷‍♂️ 2nd Builder (Optional Purchase) – If you buy him, life becomes easier. If not, your first builder will keep suffering.

⚔️ Troops Available at TH2

TH2 finally blesses you with more offense:

  • 👨‍🌾 Barbarian – Still screaming.
  • 🏹 Archer – The silent sniper. Hits from distance and pretends she’s better than Barbarians.

📚 Beginner Tip

TH2 is quick and painless — upgrade everything to the max, especially your Barracks and Army Camp. This is also the perfect time to learn good attack habits before the game starts throwing real challenges at you. Think of TH2 as “training wheels mode” for Clash of Clans 🚴.

🏰 Town Hall 2 (TH2) – Smart Early Progression

Welcome to TH2 — the stage where Clash of Clans politely whispers: “Congrats, you’ve unlocked *slightly better* problems.” 😂 This is the perfect time to set the foundation for faster growth, smarter upgrades, and fewer “Why is my gold always zero?” moments.

⚡ 1) TH2 Upgrade Roadmap (Fastest Path)

TH2 is basically the “tutorial but with taxes.” Upgrades here are extremely fast, so if you follow the right roadmap, you’ll hit TH3 before your tea gets cold. Here’s the optimal order:

  1. Army Camp → Level 2: More troops = more destruction = more loot = more happiness. Simple math. 😄
  2. Barracks → Unlock Archers: Barbarians alone feel like they skipped leg day. Archers fix that.
  3. Laboratory (when TH3): Not available yet, but save loot for later.
  4. Gold Storage & Elixir Storage: Bigger wallets = bigger upgrades soon.
  5. Clan Castle: If you can join a clan early, you’re basically upgrading with cheat codes.
  6. Walls (light upgrades): Don’t go crazy — just enough to keep baby barbarians confused.

🛠️ 2) What to Upgrade First & Why

Your #1 goal at TH2: Max offensive power first.

  • Army Camp: Every new troop slot is equal to “extra free damage.” Always upgrade first.
  • Barracks: Unlocking Archers early is a life-changer — they hit from far and complain less. 😂
  • Storages: TH3 needs resources, so prepare now.
  • Defenses (Cannon): Upgrade once your offense is sorted.

Offense → Economy → Defense. The holy triangle of early Clash wisdom. 🔺

👷 3) 1-Builder Optimization Tips

TH2 has only ONE builder… which means life is slow, painful, and full of waiting. But here’s how to squeeze max value out of that single sleepy guy:

  • Always keep the builder busy: If your builder is sleeping, your progression is sleeping too.
  • Upgrade fast-timers first when online: 5–15 min upgrades are perfect when you’re on the phone.
  • Start long upgrades before logging off: Even a 30-minute upgrade feels “long” at TH2 😆.
  • Avoid wasting loot cap: If storages are full, you’re literally losing money every minute.

⚔️ TH2 Army Guide – Troops, Attacks & Looting

🔥 4) Troops Unlock Order & Early Attack Formation

TH2 attacking is basically “Barbarians run + Archers shoot from behind.” Simple but extremely effective. Here’s the best order:

  • Barbarians: First troop, cheap, angry, and always ready for a fight.
  • Archers: The real heroes — low HP but laser-focused damage dealers.

🥇 Best TH2 Attack Combo:

10–15 Barbarians + 10 Archers

Strategy: Barbs tank in front → Archers delete everything like WiFi passwords you forget instantly. 😄

💰 Best Looting Strategy for Low Trophies

  • Stay between **0–300 trophies** for easy bases.
  • Use **full Archer snipes** to pick buildings outside defenses.
  • Target bases with exposed collectors — “free loot is the best loot.” 😎
  • Don’t chase 3-stars; farmers chase resources, not glory.

🏯 TH2 Early Base Layout Logic

🔐 5) Protecting Storages in Early Game

At TH2, storages are your kidneys — protect them at all costs. Put them in the center and let defenses guard them like they’re sacred artifacts.

  • Center your Gold Storage and Elixir Storage.
  • Surround them with your Cannon and Town Hall.
  • Use walls to make attackers walk around like they’re lost in a shopping mall.

💥 Trap Placement for Noobs (Beginner-Friendly)

Yes, traps matter even at TH2 — especially since most attackers here are on autopilot mode. 😂

  • Place traps in front of your Cannon: Catch Barbarians running in a straight line.
  • Don’t hide traps deep inside: No one will walk there — waste of a good springboard.
  • Place a trap near your storages: Surprise value = ✨ premium gameplay ✨.

🏰 Town Hall 3 (TH3) – Your First Real Progress Begins

Welcome to TH3 — the magical stage where the game finally starts feeling like Clash of Clans, not a tutorial from 2014 🤣. This is the Town Hall where your base starts looking like a base, your army stops looking like a joke, and you finally unlock enough stuff to actually attack like a pro.

6) 🛠️ TH3 Builder Management Strategy

At TH3, your biggest flex is **keeping both builders busy 24/7**. If your builder is sleeping, congratulations — you just slowed your own progress 😭.

How to Keep Builders Busy 24/7

  • Upgrade cheap stuff first: Cannons, Walls, Army Camp — these are quick wins.
  • Always leave 1 builder for small upgrades: So you don’t get stuck with a long timer.
  • Plan gold usage: Don’t let storages overflow… it hurts emotionally.
  • Upgrade Town Hall only after everything is balanced: No rushing here… yet 😆.

Storage Planning for Future Town Halls

You’ll start noticing the importance of storages now. Upgrading storages at TH3 is not optional — your Gold & Elixir needs increase massively at TH4 & TH5.

  • Upgrade Storages early — unlock higher upgrades sooner.
  • Try keeping 1 storage upgrade running at all times.
  • Never let storages sit outside the base (seriously, don’t be that guy 😭).

7) 🔬 TH3 Lab Upgrade Priority

Once the Laboratory unlocks, your troops go from “useless villagers with sticks” to “slightly more dangerous villagers with sharper sticks” ⚔️😆.


Most Useful TH3 Troop Upgrades


  1. Archers → You’ll use them forever. Worth every upgrade.
  2. Barbarians → Cheap, fast, best spam unit at low TH.
  3. Giants → Perfect tanks for early attacks.

When to Upgrade Army Camp Capacity

As soon as it becomes available. More troops = more damage = more loot = happier you 😎.

8) ⚔️ TH3 Attacking Strategy Guide

Mass Barbarians vs Mixed Army — Which Is Better?

📌 Mass Barbarians: Best for quick farming. Spam them, grab loot, leave like a ninja. Downsides: They panic when a mortar looks at them funny.

📌 Mixed Army (Giants + Archers): More controlled, stronger, safer. Giants tank damage → Archers clean everything.

Winner? Mixed Army. Barbarians are fun, but Giants + Archers are simply reliable.

Best Trophy Push Strategy at TH3

  • Use 4–6 Giants to break open defenses.
  • Spam Archers behind them for cleanup.
  • Avoid mortar-heavy bases — they delete low-level troops instantly 😭.

9) 🧱 TH3 Base Building Science

TH3 base building is where beginners make… creative mistakes 🤣. Don’t worry — here’s how to avoid them.

Anti-3 Star Layout Tips

  • Keep Town Hall inside walls — after all, it’s called Clash of Clans, not “take my TH for free.”
  • Place defenses in triangles (Cannon → Archer → Cannon).
  • Center your mortar — it’s your MVP at low levels.
  • Storages inside, collectors outside.

Avoid These Common Mistakes

  • Putting the mortar outside the walls (instant disrespect 😭).
  • Leaving gaps in walls (Giants will thank you).
  • Putting storages together — one mortar shot and you're broke.

✨ Bonus: What TH3 Players Should Focus On Before Going to TH4

  • Upgrade all defenses at least once.
  • Max Army Camps.
  • Unlock all traps — they’re cheap but game-changing.
  • Finish all lab upgrades (esp. Archers + Giants).

🏰 Town Hall 4 (TH4) – First Strategic Phase

Welcome to TH4 — the level where Clash of Clans finally stops treating you like a baby Barbarian and starts expecting you to think… just a little 😄.

🔧 10) TH4 Upgrade Priority (Maxing Roadmap)

TH4 is your first “real” upgrade phase. Maxing is easy here, but doing it in the wrong order turns your base into a free-loot buffet. So let’s fix that.

🔥 Offense vs Defense — What to Max First?

  • 1️⃣ Army Camp – More troops, more power, more fun. Simple math.
  • 2️⃣ Laboratory – Stronger troops instantly make every attack easier.
  • 3️⃣ Barracks – Unlock faster troops + Balloons (your first air unit!).
  • 4️⃣ Spell Factory – Need those Lightning Spells for Balloon plays.
  • 5️⃣ Storages – Bigger storage = bigger upgrades = less crying.
  • 6️⃣ Defenses – Archer Towers → Mortar → Air Defense → Cannon.
  • 7️⃣ Walls (late) – Do these while builders are busy. Walls at TH4 = your wrist pain era.

📘 Lab, Camp, Barracks — Perfect Upgrade Order

  • Lab: Archers → Giants → Balloons → Barbarians
  • Army Camps: Always max ASAP — huge power spike.
  • Barracks: Prioritize the one unlocking Balloons.

Pro Tip: TH4 upgrades are cheap — if you’re broke, it means you’re not attacking enough. Shame. Go attack. 😆

⚔️ 11) TH4 Best Armies for Farming & Trophy Push

🏹 Archer Rush Guide

Archer Rush is your go-to farming army. It's cheap, fast, and does not require brain cells.

  • Army: 100% Archers
  • Target: Outside collectors, unprotected storages
  • Strategy: Spread them wide — pretend you're sprinkling salt on food.

🎈 Balloon + Archer Combo (Early Air Strategy)

TH4 gives you the magic of Balloons — insanely strong if Air Defense is badly placed.

  • Army: 8–12 Balloons + Archers as cleanup
  • Use Lightning Spell to weaken Air Defense (if reachable)
  • Target: Bases with 1 Air Defense placed on the edge

Warning: If you drop Balloons into an Air Defense straight on… may the Clash gods forgive you. 😭

🛡️ 12) TH4 Defense Optimization

🎯 Air Defense Positioning Secrets

  • Place your Air Defense in the center — never outside.
  • Surround it with Storages (they’re tanky) to protect it longer.
  • Avoid “Free AD” positioning — Balloon players will thank you, but we won’t.

⚔️ Cannon | Archer Tower | Mortar — Best Placement Rules

  • Cannons: Cover ground-only paths — usually near the outer ring.
  • Archer Towers: Your most flexible defense — keep them balanced left/right.
  • Mortar: Place slightly centralized; it protects from Barch swarms.

Fun fact: A well-placed Mortar at TH4 is basically a “No Barch Allowed” sign. 😎

🏛️ 13) TH4 Base Layout Types

🧱 Anti-Barch Layout

  • Mortar covering the majority of your outer ring
  • Storages placed inside to avoid quick snipes
  • Spread-out defenses so Archers can’t wipe them all with one line

🎈 Anti-Balloon Layout

  • Centralized Air Defense
  • Archer Towers far apart to increase air coverage
  • No “open corridors” leading directly to AD

💰 Hybrid Loot Protection Layout

  • Storages evenly distributed — your gold won’t vanish in one raid
  • Town Hall inside (it’s half a storage at TH4)
  • Traps placed near high-traffic points

Reminder: No layout is perfect… unless your opponent is AFK. 😴

Clash of Clans Low-Level Progression Psychology 🤯🏰

Low-level Clash of Clans isn’t just a “beginner phase” — it’s a full mental journey. TH1–TH4 players go through emotions that even higher-level players secretly relate to. Let’s break down what actually happens inside your brain while you're still rocking Barbarians with the confidence of a TH16 pro. 😎

1) The “Everything Feels Powerful” Illusion 💪😤

At TH1–TH3, every building upgrade feels like you just unlocked nuclear technology. You upgrade your Cannon to Level 2 and suddenly you think your base is undefeatable. Spoiler: even a toddler with 10 Barbarians can destroy it. But the illusion is IMPORTANT — it boosts your motivation and makes the game fun early on.

This phase makes you believe: “More levels = more power = I am unstoppable.” And honestly? That confidence is what keeps you grinding. ❤️

2) The Obsessive Builder Syndrome (OBS) 🧱⏳

Once you unlock your second builder, your brain immediately develops a new disorder: “Builders should NEVER sleep.”

You check every 6 minutes like: 🔍 “Is my wall done yet?” 🏃 “Should I upgrade this first?” 💡 “What if I save for something bigger?”

Congratulations — you are now officially hooked on the upgrade cycle like every Clash player ever.

3) The Gold Anxiety Phase 😭💰

At low levels, one storage upgrade suddenly feels like saving money for a house. You see 30,000 Gold and your brain thinks: “I must protect this with my life.”

Every attack becomes a risky heist movie. Every raid feels like you're stealing treasure from a dragon. And every time someone steals YOUR loot, you feel personally betrayed.

4) The Base Building Overconfidence Mode 🧠📐

You place a wall. You rotate a building. You make a tiny symmetrical pattern.

And suddenly you're like: “Bro, Supercell should hire me as their new base designer.”

Meanwhile, an opponent drops 12 Barbarians and your base collapses like a Jenga tower. But hey — the confidence matters. It helps you learn layout logic faster.

5) The Early Attack Addiction ⚔️🔥

No matter how many times people say “save your army,” you ALWAYS click “Find Match” as soon as your troops finish training.

Your brain goes: ☑️ Free loot ☑️ Easy base ☑️ Barbarians ready

And boom — you’re in an attack again. Early game is literally the golden age of non-stop raiding.

6) The Progression Attachment Syndrome 💖⬆️

Every level feels emotional. Your first upgraded Archer Tower? Beautiful. Your first 100,000 Gold? Legendary. Your first Clan Castle? Emotional damage (because you had to save so much).

This attachment creates a strong habit loop: Upgrade → Wait → Reward → Upgrade again. And that's why you never quit the game… even years later.

7) The “I Will Be Maxed Soon!” Delusion 🤡🔮

Every low-level player believes they’ll max their base “super fast.” Reality hits around TH7 when you see upgrade times like “12 hours” and your hopes and dreams collapse like your storage under a Giant attack.

But in early levels? You truly believe the grind is easy… and that belief keeps you motivated. 😄

✨ Bonus: Why Low-Level Psychology Matters

These early emotions are not random — they are the core reason CoC has incredible player retention. Supercell wants you to feel powerful, emotional, attached, and slightly overconfident. That feeling drives progression, fun, and long-term engagement.

So if you're still at low levels: Enjoy the chaos! This is the most innocent, funniest, and stress-free phase of your Clash journey. 🚀🔥

TH1–4 Fastest Progress Formula (Complete Blueprint) 🚀

Welcome to the official speedrun guide for Town Hall 1 to Town Hall 4 — also known as “the phase where your base still looks like a construction site.” If you follow this formula, you'll blast through low levels faster than a Goblin chasing a free gold pile. 🤑

🔹 Phase 1: TH1–2 Speedrun Mode (Yes, Rushing Here is Correct)

At these levels, upgrading everything is like ironing your socks — no need. The goal is simple: unlock your actual village tools ASAP.

  • Upgrade Town Hall to TH2 instantly. No regrets. No shame.
  • Build basic stuff only: Army Camp, Barracks, Storage. Don’t waste time on decorating.
  • Use the free boost you get — consider it Supercell’s “starter pack pity mode.”
  • Spam attacks with Barbarians — even confused Barbarians can 3-star TH1 villages.

🔹 Phase 2: TH2 Perfect Efficiency Formula

TH2 is where beginners usually chill… but you — the future pro — are going to treat TH2 like a warm-up lap.

  • Upgrade Army Camp first → More troops = more loot = more progress.
  • Upgrade Barracks → Unlocking Archers early is a cheat code.
  • Storages next → Because TH3 upgrades cost more, and looting TH2 bases will not be enough later.
  • Keep your single builder busy 24/7. A sleeping builder is basically lost time. 😴💸
  • Attack in 30–200 trophies range → tons of abandoned bases here.

🔹 Phase 3: TH3 — The First Real Progress Point

TH3 feels like the first “real” Town Hall. You unlock more troops, more defenses, and more ways to mess up. But don’t worry — this formula removes all confusion:

✔ Your TH3 Upgrade Blueprint

  • Laboratory → First priority (Small level, big impact in attacks).
  • Army Camp → Second priority (Carry more troops = more destruction).
  • Barracks → Third (Get Giants quickly — walking tanks).
  • Storages → Always keep storage maxed before going TH4.
  • Defenses last → Especially walls (but don’t skip them entirely).

✔ Speed-Attack Method at TH3

  • Mass Barbarians for farming (literally works on auto-pilot).
  • Barb + Archer mix for trophy push.
  • Stay in **200–400 trophies** → jackpot of abandoned villages.

🔹 Phase 4: TH4 — Your First “Real Strategy Town Hall”

TH4 unlocks Balloons, Air Defense, real base designing… And also your first real chance to panic. But follow the formula:

✔ TH4 Maxing Roadmap (Fast & Clean)

  • Lab → Army Camp → Barracks → Spell Factory (best offensive order)
  • Balloons upgrade ASAP → your early-game nuke squad 💣🎈
  • Upgrade Air Defense early → because other TH4 players will also be balloon spammers.

✔ TH4 Attack Meta

  • Balloon + Archer = chef's kiss for farming 🎯
  • Pure Archers = safest method for sniping loot
  • Stay around **400–700 trophies** for perfect farming

✔ TH4 Base Building Logic

  • Protect storages like they’re your exam results.
  • AD in the center → Balloons hate walking.
  • Mortar slightly inside → Anti-Barch shield.
  • Archer Towers & Cannons → Cover each other, no lonely defenses!
